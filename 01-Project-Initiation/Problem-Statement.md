# CarePath Problem Statement

## Organisation Background

BrightCare Health is a fictional healthcare provider managing outpatient
referrals across multiple medical specialties.

Referrals are received from general practitioners and other healthcare
providers through email, scanned documents and inconsistent referral forms.

## Current-State Problem

The current referral process relies heavily on manual administrative review.
Referral officers must check whether the required patient, referrer and clinical
information has been provided before forwarding the referral for clinical review.

Incomplete referrals require staff to contact the referring provider for
additional information. Referrals may also be routed to the wrong specialty,
creating rework and increasing the time patients wait for an appointment.

Patients and referrers have limited visibility of referral status. Healthcare
operations managers also lack a consolidated view of referral volumes, waiting
times, processing delays and appointment backlogs.

## Core Problem Statement

BrightCare Health lacks a standardised and transparent process for submitting,
validating, routing and monitoring outpatient referrals.

Manual processing, inconsistent referral information and limited operational
reporting contribute to administrative rework, processing delays and poor
visibility of the patient referral journey.

## Business Impact

The current process may result in:

- Delays in referral validation and clinical review.
- Increased administrative workload.
- Repeated communication with referring providers.
- Incorrect referral routing.
- Limited visibility of patient waiting times.
- Difficulty monitoring referral backlogs.
- Inconsistent patient and referrer communication.
- Limited evidence for operational decision-making.

## Possible Root Causes

The initial root-cause hypotheses are:

- Multiple referral submission channels.
- No mandatory standard referral fields.
- Manual completeness checks.
- Manual specialty routing.
- Limited system integration.
- No central referral status tracker.
- Inconsistent patient notification processes.
- No consolidated operational dashboard.

These are working hypotheses and would need to be validated through stakeholder
interviews, process observation and data analysis.

## Opportunity Statement

BrightCare Health has an opportunity to design a centralised outpatient referral
management solution that standardises referral submission, improves information
quality, supports administrative routing, provides status visibility and enables
data-driven performance monitoring.

AI may support administrative activities such as identifying missing information
and suggesting a referral category. Final clinical decisions must remain under
the control of authorised healthcare professionals.
