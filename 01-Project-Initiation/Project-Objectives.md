# Project Objectives and Success Measures

## Primary Objective

Design a conceptual outpatient referral management solution that improves the
quality, efficiency, transparency and measurability of the referral process.

## Business Objectives

### OBJ-01: Improve referral completeness

Ensure that required administrative and referral information is captured before
a referral is submitted.

**Target:** At least 95% of submitted referrals contain all mandatory information.

### OBJ-02: Reduce validation time

Reduce the time referral officers spend checking referral completeness.

**Target:** At least 90% of referrals receive initial administrative validation
within one business day.

### OBJ-03: Reduce routing rework

Improve the accuracy of routing referrals to the appropriate specialty.

**Target:** Reduce referrals returned because of incorrect administrative routing
by 40%.

### OBJ-04: Improve referral visibility

Allow authorised users to view the current referral status and relevant
processing history.

**Target:** 100% of referrals have a visible status and audit history.

### OBJ-05: Improve communication

Provide consistent notifications when important referral or appointment events
occur.

**Target:** Notifications are generated for referral receipt, requests for
additional information, acceptance, rejection and appointment booking.

### OBJ-06: Enable operational reporting

Provide management with visibility of referral volumes, processing time, waiting
time, backlog and service performance.

**Target:** Provide a Power BI dashboard containing agreed operational KPIs.

### OBJ-07: Support responsible AI

Use AI only to support administrative review and routing.

**Target:** Every AI recommendation is recorded, explainable and confirmed or
overridden by an authorised human user.

## Important Note

The targets in this portfolio project are hypothetical. In a real project, the
Business Analyst would confirm baselines and targets with stakeholders before
approval.
