# CarePath Stakeholder Register

## Purpose

This register identifies the people and groups affected by the CarePath
outpatient referral management project. It records their responsibilities,
influence, interests and engagement needs.

| ID | Stakeholder | Role in the Process | Interest | Influence | Key Needs and Expectations | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STK-01 | Patient | Receives referral updates and attends appointments | High | Medium | Timely appointments, clear updates, privacy and accessible communication | Surveys, interviews and usability testing |
| STK-02 | Referring Clinician | Creates and submits patient referrals | High | High | Simple referral form, clear mandatory fields and referral status visibility | Interviews, workshops and prototype reviews |
| STK-03 | Referral Administration Officer | Validates referral completeness and manages referral records | High | High | Reduced manual checking, clear work queues and fewer incomplete referrals | Process workshops, observation and UAT |
| STK-04 | Clinical Review Nurse | Reviews clinical information and recommends urgency | High | High | Complete information, safe decision support and human control | Clinical workshops and requirements reviews |
| STK-05 | Specialist Clinician | Reviews accepted referrals and provides specialist care | Medium | High | Appropriate referrals, relevant clinical information and manageable workload | Interviews and solution reviews |
| STK-06 | Appointment Scheduling Officer | Books, reschedules and cancels appointments | High | Medium | Accurate referral status, appointment availability and patient contact details | Workshops and UAT |
| STK-07 | Healthcare Operations Manager | Monitors referral performance and service delivery | High | High | Dashboard visibility, reliable KPIs and backlog monitoring | Regular meetings and dashboard demonstrations |
| STK-08 | Privacy and Compliance Officer | Ensures appropriate handling of healthcare information | High | High | Privacy controls, consent, access restrictions and audit history | Compliance reviews and requirement approval |
| STK-09 | IT System Administrator | Maintains system configuration, access and availability | Medium | High | Clear technical requirements, role-based access and support procedures | Technical workshops and implementation reviews |
| STK-10 | Data and BI Analyst | Develops operational reports and monitors data quality | High | Medium | Consistent data definitions, complete records and reliable reporting fields | Data workshops and dashboard validation |
| STK-11 | Project Sponsor | Provides funding, direction and final project approval | High | High | Business value, controlled scope, measurable benefits and risk visibility | Steering meetings and status reports |
| STK-12 | Business Analyst | Elicits, analyses, documents and validates requirements | High | Medium | Stakeholder access, clear decisions and approved requirements | Workshops, interviews and document reviews |

## Stakeholder Analysis Notes

- Clinical safety decisions must remain with authorised healthcare professionals.
- Patients should be involved in accessibility and communication design.
- Privacy and compliance review must occur throughout the project.
- Referral administration and clinical review staff are key operational users.
- The project sponsor and healthcare operations manager are key decision-makers.
