# CarePath Project Scope

## Scope Statement

The project covers the outpatient referral journey from the submission of a
referral through administrative validation, clinical review, appointment
scheduling and referral closure.

The project designs the business processes, requirements, user stories,
wireframes, information model, synthetic dataset and Power BI dashboard required
for a conceptual referral management solution.

## Process Start

The process begins when a referring healthcare provider submits an outpatient
referral.

## Process End

The process ends when the referral is:

- Accepted and an appointment is scheduled.
- Returned for additional information.
- Declined with an authorised reason.
- Cancelled or closed with an audit record.

## Key Constraint

CarePath may assist with administrative validation and routing, but it will not
make diagnoses, treatment recommendations or final clinical-priority decisions.

## Project Constraints

- Only fictional and synthetic patient data will be used.
- No production healthcare system will be developed.
- No real hospital integration will be implemented.
- Clinical decisions require an authorised human reviewer.
- The portfolio project must be completed within one week.

## Assumptions

- Referring providers have access to the digital referral form.
- Medical specialties and referral categories are predefined.
- Authorised clinical staff are available to review referrals.
- Appointment availability data is accessible to scheduling staff.
- Notification preferences are captured appropriately.
