# CarePath Power–Interest Matrix

## Purpose

The Power–Interest Matrix determines how each CarePath stakeholder should be
engaged based on their level of influence and interest in the project.

| Power / Influence | Lower Interest | High Interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | **Keep Satisfied**<br><br>• Specialist Clinician<br>• IT System Administrator | **Manage Closely**<br><br>• Project Sponsor<br>• Healthcare Operations Manager<br>• Referring Clinician<br>• Referral Administration Officer<br>• Clinical Review Nurse<br>• Privacy and Compliance Officer |
| **Low or Medium** | **Monitor**<br><br>• Other non-participating hospital departments | **Keep Informed**<br><br>• Patient<br>• Appointment Scheduling Officer<br>• Data and BI Analyst<br>• Business Analyst |

## Engagement Strategies

### Manage Closely

These stakeholders are key decision-makers or operational users. They should
participate in workshops, requirement reviews, process validation and major
project decisions.

### Keep Satisfied

These stakeholders have significant influence but may not participate in daily
project activities. They should receive targeted updates and be consulted when
decisions affect their specialist or technical responsibilities.

### Keep Informed

These stakeholders are highly interested in the project outcomes. They should
receive regular updates and participate in relevant interviews, demonstrations
and usability testing.

### Monitor

These stakeholders require general awareness but do not need frequent project
communication unless the project scope or organisational impact changes.

## Key Observation

The Project Sponsor, Healthcare Operations Manager, Referral Administration
Officer and Clinical Review Nurse require close engagement because their
decisions directly affect project approval, operational processes and clinical
safety.
