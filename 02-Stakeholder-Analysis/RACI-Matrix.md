# CarePath RACI Matrix

## RACI Definitions

- **R – Responsible:** Completes the activity.
- **A – Accountable:** Owns and approves the outcome.
- **C – Consulted:** Provides input before decisions are made.
- **I – Informed:** Receives updates about the outcome.

## Stakeholder Abbreviations

- **SP:** Project Sponsor
- **OM:** Healthcare Operations Manager
- **BA:** Business Analyst
- **RA:** Referral Administration Officer
- **CR:** Clinical Review Nurse
- **PC:** Privacy and Compliance Officer
- **IT:** IT System Administrator
- **BI:** Data and BI Analyst

| Prepare and coordinate UAT | I | A | R | R | R | C | C | C |
| Review UAT results and outstanding issues | I | A | R | C | C | C | C | C |
| Approve the final proposed solution | A | R | C | I | C | C | I | I |

## RACI Principles Applied

- Every activity has only one accountable stakeholder.
- The Business Analyst is responsible for requirements and process artefacts.
- Clinical requirements remain accountable to an authorised clinical reviewer.
- Privacy requirements remain accountable to the Privacy and Compliance Officer.
- The Healthcare Operations Manager owns operational design and UAT outcomes.
- The Project Sponsor provides final project and solution approval.
