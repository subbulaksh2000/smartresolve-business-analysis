# CarePath: AI-Assisted Outpatient Referral Management

## Project Overview

CarePath is a fictional healthcare Business Analyst portfolio project focused on
improving the outpatient referral and appointment management process.

The project demonstrates healthcare process analysis, requirements engineering,
Agile delivery, responsible AI analysis and Power BI reporting.

All organisations, people and patient records used in this project are fictional.
The project uses synthetic data only.

## Business Problem

BrightCare Health receives outpatient referrals through emails, scanned documents
and inconsistent referral forms. Some referrals are incomplete, requiring staff to
contact referrers for additional information.

Manual validation and routing contribute to processing delays, limited visibility
of referral status and growing appointment backlogs. Management also lacks a
central dashboard for monitoring referral processing time, waiting time and service
performance.

## Project Objective

Design an improved referral management process and conceptual digital solution that:

- Standardises referral submission.
- Reduces incomplete referrals.
- Improves referral routing.
- Provides transparent status tracking.
- Supports responsible AI-assisted administrative review.
- Provides operational reporting through Power BI.

## Success Measures

- Reduce incomplete referral submissions by 40%.
- Reduce average referral validation time by 30%.
- Improve referrals processed within the target timeframe to 90%.
- Reduce manual referral-routing effort.
- Provide visibility of referral backlogs and patient waiting times.
- Ensure all clinical decisions remain under human control.

## Project Scope

### In Scope

- Digital outpatient referral form.
- Referral information validation.
- Referral routing to the appropriate specialty.
- Human review of clinical urgency.
- Appointment scheduling workflow.
- Referral and appointment status tracking.
- Patient notifications.
- Power BI operational dashboard.
- AI-assisted administrative recommendations.
- Privacy, security and audit requirements.

### Out of Scope

- Clinical diagnosis.
- Automated clinical decision-making.
- Medication management.
- Emergency department workflows.
- Integration with real hospital systems.
- Use of real patient information.
- Development of a production-ready healthcare application.

## Key Stakeholders

- Patient.
- General practitioner or referring clinician.
- Referral administration officer.
- Clinical review nurse.
- Specialist clinician.
- Appointment scheduling officer.
- Healthcare operations manager.
- Privacy and compliance officer.
- IT system administrator.
- Business analyst.

## Responsible AI Principle

The proposed AI capability may identify missing referral information, recommend an
administrative category and flag referrals for review. It must not independently
diagnose a patient, determine final clinical urgency or make treatment decisions.
